## Limits of this record

* The scanner finds known canaries, listed shapes and names. It does not find an arbitrary secret, anything it cannot unpack, or live memory; see the blind-spot list in the [guide](../canary-scan.md#blind-spots).
* Exposure by design (the consumer process holds the secret; the controller sees metadata; sessions have access) is a reviewer's decision, recorded only through allowlist reasons. One allowance exists so far.
* The scanner does not verify backups or images; `blindpass backup verify` and the SBOM/attestation gates do.
* Timing: about 15 MB/s of decompressed text/binary content with all checks on a loaded host, far faster over sparse memory (all-zero chunks are counted and skipped; the Chromium cores are 32 GiB each).
* No dependency was added: the scanner uses the Python standard library; PyYAML is used by `check-core-limits.py` only and fails closed when absent.
